01 · Manifesto

Every era leaves behind a language. This is ours.

Before the protocols, the chains, the wallets — there were words. Hodl. Wagmi. Genesis. Sovereign. Strange syllables passed between strangers in encrypted channels, then chanted in unison across continents.

Crypto Lexicon is a curated pantheon of 146 words that defined this movement — sorted into seven sacred categories, each token forged with its own palette, material, geometry, and aura.

This is not a PFP. It is a relic. A word, made object, made eternal.
